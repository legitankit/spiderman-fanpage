THE SPIDER-MAN

MOVIE CHRONICLES

Every movie tells a different chapter of Spider-Man's journey — from discovering who he is to learning what it truly means to be a hero.

2002

SPIDER-MAN

THE BEGINNING OF A HERO

Released in 2002, Spider-Man tells the origin story of Peter Parker and how an ordinary teenager becomes Spider-Man after being bitten by a genetically altered spider.

Peter learns that having extraordinary abilities also comes with extraordinary responsibility. After losing Uncle Ben, he understands that his powers are not meant only for himself.

What Peter learns: Responsibility is not about what you can do, but about what you choose to do with your power.

2004

SPIDER-MAN 2

THE HERO WHO WANTED TO GIVE UP

Released in 2004, Spider-Man 2 explores Peter Parker's struggle between his personal life and his responsibility as Spider-Man.

Peter begins to lose his powers because he is emotionally exhausted and unsure about the life he has chosen. Doctor Octopus becomes a powerful threat while Peter struggles to decide whether he can continue being a hero.

What Peter learns: Being a hero is difficult, but running away from responsibility does not make the problems disappear.

2007

SPIDER-MAN 3

THE BATTLE WITHIN

Released in 2007, Spider-Man 3 explores Peter's darker side after the mysterious black symbiote attaches itself to him.

The symbiote amplifies Peter's anger, arrogance and desire for revenge. While facing Sandman, Harry Osborn and Venom, Peter must also confront the worst parts of himself.

What Peter learns: Sometimes the hardest enemy is not standing in front of you — it is the darkness growing inside you.

2012

THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN

DISCOVERING THE TRUTH

Released in 2012, The Amazing Spider-Man introduces Andrew Garfield as Peter Parker.

Peter investigates the mystery surrounding his parents while discovering his new abilities. At the same time, he develops a relationship with Gwen Stacy and faces Dr. Curt Connors, who becomes the Lizard.

What Peter learns: Understanding where you come from can help you understand who you want to become.

2014

THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2

LOVE, LOSS & RESPONSIBILITY

Released in 2014, The Amazing Spider-Man 2 follows Peter as he struggles to balance his relationship with Gwen while facing Electro and Harry Osborn.

Peter discovers more about his father's past but also experiences one of the most painful losses of his life. Gwen's death forces Peter to confront the cost of being Spider-Man.

What Peter learns: Some losses cannot be undone. You can only carry the memory forward and find the strength to keep going.

2017

SPIDER-MAN: HOMECOMING

YOU DON'T NEED A SUIT TO BE A HERO

Released in 2017, Spider-Man: Homecoming follows a young Peter Parker trying to prove himself after meeting Tony Stark.

Peter becomes obsessed with becoming an important hero and goes after Adrian Toomes, the Vulture, even when he is warned to stay away.

What Peter learns: A hero is defined by his choices, not by a powerful suit or someone else's approval.

2019

SPIDER-MAN: FAR FROM HOME

GROWING BEYOND EXPECTATIONS

Released in 2019, Far From Home follows Peter after the events of Avengers: Endgame. He wants a normal school trip but finds himself pulled into another global threat.

Mysterio manipulates Peter's trust and uses illusions to make himself appear heroic. Peter eventually learns to trust his own instincts.

What Peter learns: Growing up means learning to trust yourself when there is no one else to tell you what to do.

2021

SPIDER-MAN: NO WAY HOME

THE GREATEST SACRIFICE

Released in 2021, No Way Home brings Peter face-to-face with villains and Spider-Men from other universes after a spell goes wrong.

Peter wants to save everyone, but his attempt comes with devastating consequences. After losing Aunt May, Peter understands what being Spider-Man truly demands.

What Peter learns: True heroism sometimes means sacrificing your own happiness so that others can have a better future.

2026

SPIDER-MAN: BRAND NEW DAY

A NEW BEGINNING

Brand New Day follows Peter Parker as he starts a new chapter after the events that changed his life. With his past left behind, Peter must continue protecting the city while trying to find his place in a world that has moved on.

A new threat pushes Peter to confront difficult choices and reminds him that being Spider-Man is more than wearing a mask. He must once again choose between an ordinary life and the responsibility that comes with being a hero.

What Peter learns: Every ending can become the beginning of a stronger hero.
