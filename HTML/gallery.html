SPIDER-MEN

POWERS: Super Strength, Spider-Sense, Wall-Crawling, Agility and Organic Web-Shooting.

EARTH-120703

Peter Parker

A brilliant and emotionally driven Spider-Man whose story was shaped by love, loss and redemption.

MOVIES: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN (2012), THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2 (2014)

POWERS: Super Strength, Spider-Sense, Wall-Crawling, Agility and Web-Shooting.

ORIGIN: Peter Parker is bitten by a genetically modified spider at Oscorp, gaining enhanced abilities and becoming Spider-Man.

PORTRAYED BY: Andrew Garfield

Peter Parker

A young hero who grows from a neighborhood Spider-Man into one of Earth's greatest defenders.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN: HOMECOMING (2017), SPIDER-MAN: FAR FROM HOME (2019), SPIDER-MAN: NO WAY HOME (2021), SPIDER-MAN: BRAND NEW DAY (2026)

POWERS: Super Strength, Spider-Sense, Wall-Crawling, Agility, Enhanced Reflexes and Web-Shooting.

Miles Morales

A new generation of Spider-Man with unique powers, a different story and his own leap of faith.

POWERS: Super Strength, Spider-Sense, Wall-Crawling, Camouflage, Venom Blast and Web-Shooting.

Ben Reilly

COMICS: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN, THE CLONE SAGA

02

VILLAINS

OSCORP

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn's twisted transformation created one of Spider-Man's most dangerous enemies.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN (2002)

ORIGIN: Norman Osborn experiments with an unstable performance-enhancing formula on himself. The experiment causes a violent split in his personality, creating the Green Goblin.

OCTAVIUS INDUSTRIES

Doctor Octopus

Dr. Otto Octavius became a powerful enemy after his mechanical arms took control of his mind and body.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN 2 (2004)

ORIGIN: Otto Octavius creates four mechanical arms for a fusion experiment. When the experiment goes wrong, the neural inhibitor is destroyed and the arms begin influencing his mind, transforming him into Doctor Octopus.

HARRY OSBORN

New Goblin

Harry Osborn takes on the mantle of the Green Goblin, becoming Spider-Man's enemy while carrying the legacy of his father.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN 3 (2007)

ORIGIN: After discovering that Peter Parker was responsible for his father's death, Harry uses his father's technology and enhanced equipment to become the New Goblin.

EDDIE BROCK

Venom

Eddie Brock becomes bonded with an alien symbiote, creating a powerful enemy driven by hatred for Spider-Man.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN 3 (2007)

ORIGIN: An alien symbiote arrives on Earth and bonds with Peter Parker before being rejected. It later bonds with Eddie Brock, combining with him to create Venom.

FLINT MARKO

Sandman

Flint Marko gained the ability to transform his body into living sand, becoming one of Spider-Man's most powerful enemies.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN 3 (2007)

ORIGIN: Flint Marko accidentally falls into experimental particle equipment while escaping from the police. The experiment fuses his body with sand, giving him the ability to control and reshape his form.

MAX DILLON

Electro

Max Dillon was transformed into a living electrical force, gaining immense power and becoming a dangerous enemy of Spider-Man.

MOVIE: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2 (2014)

ORIGIN: Max Dillon falls into a tank filled with genetically engineered electric eels while working at Oscorp. The accident transforms his body into a powerful source of electricity, creating Electro.

CURT CONNORS

Lizard

Dr. Curt Connors' experiment to regrow his lost arm transformed him into a powerful creature driven by instinct and rage.

MOVIE: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN (2012)

ORIGIN: Dr. Curt Connors develops a serum using reptilian genetics to regenerate his missing arm. After testing it on himself, the serum transforms him into the monstrous Lizard.

ADRIAN TOOMES

Vulture

Adrian Toomes uses advanced flight technology to become a ruthless criminal and a dangerous enemy of Spider-Man.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: HOMECOMING (2017)

ORIGIN: Adrian Toomes loses his salvage business after advanced alien technology becomes controlled by the government. He begins stealing and modifying this technology, eventually creating the Vulture suit.

QUENTIN BECK

Mysterio

Quentin Beck uses advanced technology, deception and illusions to create an entirely different kind of threat.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: FAR FROM HOME (2019)

ORIGIN: Quentin Beck is a former Stark Industries employee who uses advanced drone technology, holograms and special effects to create the illusion of a powerful superhero named Mysterio.

WILSON FISK

Kingpin

Wilson Fisk is a powerful crime lord whose immense strength, influence and ruthless ambition make him a formidable enemy.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018)

ORIGIN: Wilson Fisk is a powerful crime lord who uses a particle collider in an attempt to bring back alternate versions of his family, causing the multiverse to collide and drawing Spider-People into his world.

ALEKSEI SYTSEVICH

Rhino

A dangerous criminal who becomes a heavily armored enemy of Spider-Man, using powerful technology to cause destruction.

MOVIE: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2

ORIGIN: Aleksei Sytsevich was a Russian criminal who gained powerful mechanical armor, transforming him into the Rhino.

AARON DAVIS

Prowler

A skilled and intimidating criminal who hunts Miles Morales while secretly hiding a personal connection to the young hero.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018)

ORIGIN: Aaron Davis is a talented criminal who operates as the Prowler, using an advanced combat suit and high-tech equipment to carry out dangerous missions.

DR. JONATHAN OHN

The Spot

A mysterious villain whose body is covered in interdimensional portals, allowing him to manipulate space and attack from unexpected places.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: ACROSS THE SPIDER-VERSE (2023)

ORIGIN: Dr. Jonathan Ohn is transformed after the destruction of Kingpin's collider experiment. His body becomes covered in interdimensional spots, giving him the ability to create and control portals.

SCORPION

Scorpion

A dangerous and heavily armed criminal who serves as one of Kingpin's powerful enforcers.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018)

ORIGIN: Scorpion is a criminal enhanced with a powerful mechanical suit and cybernetic equipment, giving him increased strength and deadly combat abilities.

DR. OLIVIA OCTAVIUS

Doctor Octopus

A brilliant scientist who uses advanced mechanical arms and becomes one of the most dangerous enemies in Miles Morales' universe.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018)

ORIGIN: Olivia Octavius is a brilliant scientist working for Alchemax. She develops advanced mechanical tentacles and uses them to become the Doctor Octopus of her universe.

LOTHONOMAS TOMBSTONE

Tombstone

A powerful and intimidating enforcer who works for Kingpin, known for his incredible strength and durability.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018)

ORIGIN: Tombstone is a powerful criminal and one of Kingpin's enforcers, using his immense strength and durability to carry out dangerous missions.

01

SUITS

CLASSIC

Classic Spider-Man Suit

The iconic red and blue Spider-Man suit that became the foundation for one of the most recognizable superhero designs.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN (2002), SPIDER-MAN 2 (2004), SPIDER-MAN 3 (2007)

FEATURES: A red-and-blue design featuring black web patterns, a spider emblem and a full-face mask that conceals Peter Parker's identity.

SIGNIFICANCE: The suit represents Peter's original identity as Spider-Man and his commitment to using his abilities to protect others.

WORN BY: Tobey Maguire

SYMBIOTE

Black Suit

A living alien symbiote that bonds with Peter Parker, creating a powerful black suit that enhances his abilities while affecting his emotions and behavior.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN 3 (2007)

FEATURES: The symbiote amplifies Peter's strength, agility and web-slinging abilities while forming a sleek black version of his Spider-Man suit.

ORIGIN: The symbiote arrives on Earth inside a meteorite and bonds with Peter. As its influence grows, Peter becomes more aggressive and arrogant before realizing he must separate himself from it.

WORN BY: Tobey Maguire

STARK TECH

Iron Spider

An advanced Spider-Man suit designed by Tony Stark, combining high-tech armor with Peter Parker's natural abilities.

MOVIES: AVENGERS: INFINITY WAR (2018), AVENGERS: ENDGAME (2019), SPIDER-MAN: NO WAY HOME (2021)

FEATURES: The suit includes enhanced durability, advanced web-shooters, environmental protection, an onboard AI system and four mechanical spider legs known as waldoes.

ORIGIN: Tony Stark designs the suit as an upgrade for Peter Parker. Peter first uses it during the battle against Thanos and later wears it during the events of the multiverse crisis.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

HOMEMADE • DIY

Homemade Suit

Peter Parker's first homemade Spider-Man costume, created from ordinary clothing and basic materials before he gains access to advanced superhero technology.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: HOMECOMING (2017)

FEATURES: A simple fabric-based costume with a red hoodie, blue pants, homemade web-shooters and a basic mask designed to hide Peter's identity.

ORIGIN: Peter creates the suit himself while secretly fighting crime around Queens. It represents the early stage of his journey before Tony Stark provides him with a more advanced Spider-Man suit.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

STARK TECH

Stark Suit

Peter Parker's first high-tech Spider-Man suit, equipped with advanced technology designed by Tony Stark.

MOVIES: CAPTAIN AMERICA: CIVIL WAR (2016), SPIDER-MAN: HOMECOMING (2017)

FEATURES: The suit includes enhanced web-shooters, multiple web types, a communication system, tracking technology and advanced lenses with different visual functions.

ORIGIN: Tony Stark provides Peter with the suit after recruiting him to help during the airport battle. Peter later uses it while protecting Queens as Spider-Man.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

UPGRADED • SPIDER-MAN

Upgraded Suit

An improved Spider-Man suit that combines Peter's classic design with advanced technology and greater functionality.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: FAR FROM HOME (2019)

FEATURES: The suit provides improved mobility and durability while retaining advanced web-shooters and Peter's technological abilities.

ORIGIN: Peter receives the upgraded suit during his mission in Europe after his original Stark suit is damaged. He uses it during the final battle against Mysterio.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

BLACK • RED

Black & Red Suit

A redesigned Spider-Man suit featuring a striking black and red color scheme that becomes Peter's primary costume after his European adventure.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN: FAR FROM HOME (2019), SPIDER-MAN: NO WAY HOME (2021)

FEATURES: A black base with red accents, upgraded web-shooters and improved flexibility, allowing Peter to move freely during combat.

ORIGIN: Peter creates this suit using materials and technology available to him after his Stark suit is damaged. He uses it during the final events of Far From Home.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

INTEGRATED • STARK TECH

Integrated Suit

A powerful hybrid suit that combines Peter's black-and-red Spider-Man suit with advanced Iron Spider technology.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: NO WAY HOME (2021)

FEATURES: The suit combines Peter's upgraded web-shooters with Iron Spider technology, including enhanced protection and the mechanical spider legs.

ORIGIN: Peter integrates parts of the Iron Spider technology into his existing suit during the multiverse crisis, creating a more advanced hybrid costume for his battles against the returning villains.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

ANTI-OCK • NANOTECH

Anti-Ock Suit

A specialized Spider-Man suit created from advanced nanotechnology to help Peter fight Doctor Octopus and survive the multiverse crisis.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: NO WAY HOME (2021)

FEATURES: The suit uses nanotechnology to provide enhanced protection and allows Peter to control and manipulate parts of the suit during combat.

ORIGIN: Peter uses the nanotechnology from his Iron Spider suit to create a new configuration specifically suited for his battle with Doctor Octopus.

WORN BY: Tom Holland

ADVANCED • EARTH-120703

Advanced Suit

Peter Parker's refined Spider-Man suit featuring a bold red-and-blue design, large expressive eyes and a distinctive spider emblem.

MOVIE: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2 (2014)

FEATURES: A flexible fabric design with improved mobility, expressive lenses, reinforced construction and mechanically operated web-shooters.

ORIGIN: Peter improves his original Spider-Man costume, creating a more refined suit that allows him to move efficiently while protecting New York as Spider-Man.

WORN BY: Andrew Garfield

EARTH-1610 • SPIDER-MAN

Miles Morales Suit

Miles Morales' iconic black-and-red Spider-Man suit, representing a new generation of Spider-Man and his own unique identity as a hero.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018), SPIDER-MAN: ACROSS THE SPIDER-VERSE (2023)

FEATURES: A black suit with red web patterns and a large red spider emblem, designed to complement Miles' unique abilities such as camouflage and bio-electric venom blasts.

ORIGIN: Miles initially wears Peter Parker's Spider-Man suit before creating his own black-and-red version, symbolizing his acceptance of the Spider-Man identity.

WORN BY: Shameik Moore (voice)

EARTH-65 • SPIDER-WOMAN

Spider-Gwen Suit

Gwen Stacy's distinctive Spider-Woman costume, combining a white, black and pink design with a sleek and athletic look.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018), SPIDER-MAN: ACROSS THE SPIDER-VERSE (2023)

FEATURES: A white hood, black-and-white bodysuit, pink and blue accents, flexible materials and web-shooters designed for agile movement.

ORIGIN: Gwen creates and wears the suit after becoming the Spider-hero of Earth-65, using her abilities to protect her universe while keeping her identity hidden.

WORN BY: Gwen Stacy / Spider-Woman

MULTIVERSE

EARTH-616

The Main Universe

The primary reality of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, where Peter Parker becomes Spider-Man and encounters heroes, villains and events connected to the wider MCU.

SPIDER-MAN: HOMECOMING (2017) • FAR FROM HOME (2019) • NO WAY HOME (2021)

SPIDER-MAN: Peter Parker, portrayed by Tom Holland, is the Spider-Man of this universe.

IMPORTANCE: This universe becomes central to the multiverse story in Spider-Man: No Way Home, when characters from other Spider-Man realities enter Peter's world.

EARTH-96283

Tobey Maguire's Universe

The universe where Peter Parker becomes Spider-Man and faces some of his most iconic enemies while learning the true meaning of responsibility.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN (2002), SPIDER-MAN 2 (2004), SPIDER-MAN 3 (2007)

SPIDER-MAN: Peter Parker, portrayed by Tobey Maguire, gains his abilities after being bitten by a genetically engineered spider.

IMPORTANCE: This universe is one of the major live-action Spider-Man realities and returns in Spider-Man: No Way Home as part of the multiverse.

EARTH-120703

Andrew Garfield's Universe

The universe where Peter Parker becomes Spider-Man while uncovering the secrets surrounding his parents and Oscorp.

MOVIES: THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN (2012), THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2 (2014)

SPIDER-MAN: Peter Parker, portrayed by Andrew Garfield, gains his abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider at Oscorp.

IMPORTANCE: This universe becomes part of the multiverse events in Spider-Man: No Way Home, bringing Andrew's Peter Parker into the MCU.

EARTH-1610 • ULTIMATE UNIVERSE

Miles Morales' Universe

The alternate reality where Miles Morales becomes Spider-Man and carries the legacy of Peter Parker into a new generation.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018), SPIDER-MAN: ACROSS THE SPIDER-VERSE (2023)

SPIDER-MAN: Miles Morales becomes Spider-Man after being bitten by a genetically enhanced spider.

IMPORTANCE: Miles' journey becomes one of the central stories of the Spider-Verse, showing that anyone can become Spider-Man.

EARTH-65 • SPIDER-WOMAN

Gwen Stacy's Universe

The universe where Gwen Stacy becomes the Spider-hero known as Spider-Woman, protecting her reality while dealing with the loss of her best friend.

MOVIES: SPIDER-MAN: INTO THE SPIDER-VERSE (2018), SPIDER-MAN: ACROSS THE SPIDER-VERSE (2023)

SPIDER-HERO: Gwen Stacy, voiced by Hailee Steinfeld, is the Spider-Woman of Earth-65.

IMPORTANCE: Earth-65 becomes an important reality within the Spider-Verse and Gwen plays a major role in Miles Morales' multiversal journey.

EARTH-928 • YEAR 2099

Spider-Man 2099's Universe

A futuristic Spider-Man reality set in the year 2099, where Miguel O'Hara becomes the powerful Spider-Man 2099.

MOVIE: SPIDER-MAN: ACROSS THE SPIDER-VERSE (2023)

SPIDER-MAN: Miguel O'Hara, voiced by Oscar Isaac, becomes Spider-Man 2099 and leads the Spider-Society.

IMPORTANCE: Miguel plays a major role in protecting the stability of the Spider-Verse and enforcing rules designed to prevent dangerous changes to established Spider-Man realities.

EARTH-90214 • NOIR UNIVERSE
